Flagship -- Alonsera

CRM enrichment engine

Alonsera's contact base was a manual, ungoverned scramble. I built the infrastructure that made a ~13K-record CRM clean, segmentable, and repeatable -- with zero data loss.

14,900+contacts processed at peak
3,700 → 3,558raw rows deduped into clean records
100%person-region accuracy on an n=100 QA pass
4sources merged into one schema
  • A single-page HTML/JS app on the Anthropic API -- drag-and-drop CSV in and out, progress bar, pause/resume, and a confidence + reasoning column on every row
  • Deterministic-first logic -- domain and title decide region and industry before any lookup; low-confidence rows get tagged, never guessed
  • A dedup pipeline in Python/pandas -- union schema with source tags, union-find matching on email, LinkedIn, and name-company, conservative resolution with a full log
  • A safe HubSpot round-trip -- update-only, matched on record ID, batched, preserving engagement history and owners
